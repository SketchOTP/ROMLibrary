# Implement workflow

Verify the active goal/directive and actual worktree. Inspect relevant source, callers, interfaces and tests. For any task, check its proportionate consequence triage; when a material new obligation or contradiction emerges, report it to the Conductor rather than silently expanding scope or declaring completion. Reuse sound existing code; make the smallest correct scoped change. Check proportionately, inspect the diff, and submit changed paths, acceptance results, evidence and limitations. The Conductor accepts material work; Scribe updates changed records/map facts. A small change does not require every specialist.
